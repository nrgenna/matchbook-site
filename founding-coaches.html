Founding Coach Program

Your session changes their mind. Matchbook makes it stick.

A good coaching call changes a player's thinking in sixty minutes. Then twenty hours of solo queue undo it. Matchbook lives in those twenty hours — and we want a small group of coaches to build it with us.

What it does for your students

Matchbook is a deliberate-practice system for League on Mac: it records their games, lets them flag key moments mid-game with one hotkey, and gives them a structured journal to review afterward.

For you, that means students who flag their own moments instead of forgetting them, journal between sessions instead of just queueing, and show up to the next call with specific timestamps instead of "idk, I just lost." The homework does itself — you spend the hour coaching, not reconstructing what happened.

What Founding Coaches get

Founding Coaches are co-architects of the methodology, not promoters. Here's the deal:

Lifetime free Pro access

Whatever the paid tier ends up being — pricing isn't final — Founding Coaches never pay for it.

Founding Coach status, in-app

Listed inside Matchbook as a recommended coach: your name, photo, and channel link, in front of players actively working on their game.

Direct roadmap input

Your feedback shapes what gets built. Features and changelog entries can carry the name of the coach who suggested them.

Affiliate early access

First access to the affiliate program when it opens, on the best terms it will ever have.

Co-branded case studies

Opportunities to build content together around real student results — your coaching, documented.

Who it's for

Coaches with active students — whether you're building your first roster or you've been coaching independently for years. Role and rank specialization don't matter.

What matters: your students play on Mac (or you have a few who do), and you're annoyed by the same thing we are — players nod along in the session, then queue up and do the exact same thing anyway. If retention between sessions is the problem you want solved, you're who we want on the phone.

What we ask of you

  • One 30-minute feedback call — how you coach, where students fall off, what would actually help.
  • Try Matchbook with a few of your students during the beta.
  • Honest feedback — what works, what doesn't, what's missing. Blunt is better.
  • That's it. No posting requirements, no promotion quota. If you never mention us publicly, you're still a Founding Coach.
