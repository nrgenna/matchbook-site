For the work between coaching calls

Make the next hour
start further along.

Bring the moment. Bring the thinking. Give the next coaching conversation something specific to work with.

Keep the lesson
close to the game.

A player can understand a lesson on a call and lose track of it in the next queue. Matchbook gives them a place to set a focus, save the moment, and write down what happened when they tried.

Start with a timestamp.

A flagged decision is easier to discuss than a vague memory of a bad game. Revisit it with the recording close by.

Bring a useful clip.

Trim and export the part that matters. Players choose what to share through the channels they already use.

Keep their thinking.

Player-written notes show the intention behind a decision. Turn that into a specific focus for the next session.
