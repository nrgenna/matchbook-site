Good questions

Before you
queue up.

A few things worth knowing about where Matchbook is today.

Can I download Matchbook today?

There is no public download yet. Matchbook is in development. Email hello@matchbook.gg to ask about availability. This opens your email app; it does not sign you up automatically.

What does recording need?

A supported Mac, the League client, and macOS Screen Recording permission. Recording starts automatically when supported games begin after setup. Global hotkeys also need Accessibility permission. See the recording guide.

Can I keep my existing recordings?

You can import video files for review. Matchbook can remain part of your practice routine if you already record elsewhere. Capture compatibility and timeline alignment depend on the file and setup; importing a video is separate from live hotkey flagging.

Is this an AI coach?

No. The current product helps you review and reflect on your own decisions. Automated AI coaching is not part of the current product.

Do I need a Matchbook account?

The core Mac workflow uses local data. An account is optional for multi-device sync. Cloud clips and Companion access have their own setup. The privacy policy explains what is stored where.

Does this work for coaching?

Players can bring specific moments, exported clips, and written takeaways to a coaching session. Dedicated coach dashboards and a marketplace are not part of the current product. See the coaching workflow.
